PIPER VOICE DATASET — READING SCRIPT
=====================================
How to use:
- Set Capture tab device to your MIC, Speaker = your name, Buffer ~10s.
- Click Record Now, read ONE numbered line clearly, let it save. Repeat.
- Quiet room, steady distance, natural pace. Don't rush the ends of sentences.
- After recording, FIX each transcript to match exactly what you said.
- ~150-200 clean lines is a strong dataset.

Read naturally — like you're talking in a call, not like a news anchor.
